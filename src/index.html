        How this works

        Remember the position, hide the pieces, then find the piece named above the board. Start reveals the position. The Solve timer starts after the pieces disappear. Expert asks you to recall three pieces from a denser position. Turn Timer off in More for untimed study.

        A clean solve means all answers were right without hints, retries or showing the position. With Timer on, finish before time runs out. Your best result and unfinished sessions stay in this browser.

        This is a route puzzle: only the White knight moves. Black’s pieces stay still. Reach the marked goal using empty squares that Black does not attack.

        Several shortest routes can be correct. A clean solve uses a shortest safe route without hints, retries, wrong attempts or Undo. With Timer on, finish before time runs out. Expert routes take at least eight hops, with landing hints hidden. Hints are calculated from your current square. Timed positions stay hidden until Start and while paused. If time runs out you can keep practicing. Pause and Timer on/off are in More.
