Lesson notes +
A five-step calculation routine
  1. Count the material and identify the opponent’s immediate threats.
  2. List forcing checks, captures, and direct attacks.
  3. Choose a candidate and calculate plausible defensive replies.
  4. Compare the resulting position with the one you started from.
  5. Recheck the line for hidden resources before committing.
