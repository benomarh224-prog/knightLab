# Knightlab academy artwork

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the user-approved homepage reference direction. No external stock images or text embedded in the pictures. The original generated PNGs are copied into this directory and remain unchanged. Website typography, controls, chess puzzle, progress, and path markers are implemented as HTML/CSS/JavaScript.

Final saved assets and prompt briefs:

- `src/assets/academy-hero.png`: Widescreen 2.4:1 premium cinematic chess background. A polished ivory Staunton knight and taller obsidian king on the right, on dark green marble in a lush classical conservatory. Warm sunlight from the upper right and dark empty space on the left for the real heading. No text, logo, buttons, or UI.
- `src/assets/french-defense.png`: Landscape 3:2 luxury course-cover still life. A polished obsidian bishop beside a smaller ivory pawn on a reflective green marble chessboard, with sunlit arches and foliage in the background. Realistic carved details, warm highlights, forest green shadows. No text or UI.
- `src/assets/tactical-vision.png`: Landscape 3:2 tactical study artwork. A translucent lime glass Staunton knight and restrained glowing paths on a reflective dark green marble chessboard. Blurred dark pieces, warm classical conservatory, high-end realistic product photography. No text or UI.
- `src/assets/chess-play.png`: Landscape 3:2 ivory king and pawn on a green and cream marble chessboard in a warm sunlit stone arcade. Realistic luxury still life, forest shadows and honey ivory highlights. No text or UI.
- `src/assets/learning-journey.png`: Very wide cinematic learning-path landscape. Four mossy rocky plateaus and small stone platforms above conifer forests and misty mountain ridges. Deep forest greens, a faint lime trail, dark upper and lower space for website content. No chess pieces, numbers, icons, or UI.

The images are decorative editorial illustrations. They do not assert evaluated chess positions. The course cards link to the French Defense course, Tactical Vision course, and Play respectively. The French learning path and the Tactical Vision course each use their own stored practice progress.

`open-games.svg` is an original code-generated vector cover, using Knightlab’s Staunton piece SVGs and the legal position after `1. e4 e5 2. Nf3 Nc6 3. Bc4 Bc5`. It is not an illustration copied from the supplied book.

The current opening-course covers are the user-supplied `italian-game-cover.jpg` and `french-defense-cover.jpg`, copied unchanged from their attached images. They replace the earlier cover references in the catalog; the French cover also illustrates the homepage Openings category. Their embedded headings are preserved using `object-fit: contain`, with a full 3:2 image area on phones.
