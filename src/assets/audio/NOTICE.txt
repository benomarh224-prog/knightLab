Recorded chess-piece sounds (CC0 1.0), edited for ROQVYN.

chess_move_on_alabaster.wav by mh2o
https://freesound.org/people/mh2o/sounds/351518/
Chess piece setting 1 (queen on a wooden board) by Alnotreally
https://freesound.org/people/Alnotreally/sounds/801349/

Public HQ previews were excerpted, converted to mono, filtered, normalized,
faded and resampled to 24 kHz. Event sounds mix these two recordings; no
oscillators, harsh beeps, pitch shifts or Chess.com/Chessigma audio are used.
